I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by [Prof. Yuanbo Xiangli](https://kam1107.github.io/). My research focuses on robot learning, particularly humanoid loco-manipulation and world models for robotic interaction.

## Research interests

- **Humanoid loco-manipulation:** learning coordinated whole-body and dexterous manipulation skills from human demonstrations.
- **World models:** learning representations and predictive models to support robotic behavior.

## Selected project

**DexWeave** — DexWeave combines interaction-consistent whole-body retargeting with anatomy-aware reinforcement learning to enable dexterous humanoid loco-manipulation from human demonstrations. [Project Page](https://dexweave.github.io) [Arxiv](https://arxiv/com) [Paper](https://dexweave.github.io/dexweave.pdf)
